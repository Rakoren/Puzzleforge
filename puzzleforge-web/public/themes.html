How it works

  1. Pick a topic. Anything concrete works best — an animal group, a place, a hobby, a school subject.
  2. Generate. Claude builds three difficulty levels of real words, each with a short clue, so the same theme works for easy kids' puzzles and harder adult ones.
  3. Review. Check the words and counts. The offensive-word filter has already removed anything inappropriate.
  4. Save. The theme joins your library and shows up everywhere you pick a theme — word searches, crosswords, scrambles, and full books.

Generated themes are saved as ordinary theme files, so you can edit or remove them later just like the built-in ones.
