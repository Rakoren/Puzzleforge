Pages

    Use ↑ ↓ to reorder, ⧉ to duplicate, ✕ to delete. Insert blank pages to keep coloring/drawing backs empty and lay out your adventure book by hand.

    Open a book from the Book Builder (“Open in Editor”), or use Open recipe above to load a saved book.

    Drag to move; ○ handle resizes. Arrow keys nudge (Shift = 10px). Ctrl+Z/Y undo/redo, Ctrl+C/V/D copy/paste/duplicate. Export composites everything at print resolution.

    Insert page template

    Drops a new page after the current one. Everything is editable — type over the text, move it, or delete what you don't want.

    Runs a pre-flight check and proofread, then bundles everything KDP needs into one download. Nothing here changes your book — you can export even with warnings.
